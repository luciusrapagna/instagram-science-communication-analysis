# Example CPIF Mapping for the Instagram Extension Study

## Layer 1

Two university extension projects used Instagram for organic science communication under the same institutional environment and comparable weekly publication frequency. They differed in theme, audience, account history, follower base, and format distribution.

## Layer 2

- Exposure: views and reach
- Interaction: likes, comments, shares, saves, total interactions
- Audience growth: follows
- Derived indicators: reach-normalised interaction rates

## Layer 3

Use `03_audit_analytical_variables.py` to verify analytical variables, categories, missingness, and metric consistency.

## Layer 4

Use scripts 04–09 for project comparisons, format comparisons, stratified analyses, normalised rates, Spearman correlations, and outlier sensitivity.

## Layer 5

Interpret project differences as contextual performance profiles, not causal effects of project identity. Interpret format differences as observational associations. Separate exposure findings from interaction findings.

## Layer 6

Prioritise formats associated with higher interaction while preserving content-objective alignment. Continue monitoring exposure and interaction separately. Add qualitative audience feedback and outcome measures before claiming learning or social impact.
