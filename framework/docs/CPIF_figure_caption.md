# CPIF conceptual figure caption

**Figure. Communication Performance Interpretation Framework (CPIF).**

The framework organizes the context-sensitive interpretation of platform-recorded communication metrics into six connected layers: (1) Communication Context; (2) Platform-Recorded Metrics; (3) Metric Preparation and Standardization; (4) Statistical Integration; (5) Communication-Performance Interpretation; and (6) Evidence-Informed Decision Support. Platform-recorded metrics are classified into exposure, interaction, and other platform-specific indicators. Raw metrics are audited and may be transformed into normalized metrics and comparable analytical outcomes when definitions and denominators are valid. Statistical methods are selected according to the study design, distribution of the data, sampling structure, dependence between observations, and research questions. Interpretation integrates effect magnitude, uncertainty, associations, robustness, and institutional, editorial, and platform context without producing a universal score. CPIF directly evaluates communication exposure and interaction but does not directly measure learning, trust, scientific literacy, behavioural change, or societal impact. These effectiveness outcomes require outcome-specific measures and appropriate study designs.
