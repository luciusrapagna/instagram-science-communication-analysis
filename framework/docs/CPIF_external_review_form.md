# CPIF external scientific review form

## Review purpose

This form supports independent scientific and methodological review of the Communication Performance Interpretation Framework (CPIF). The review should assess conceptual coherence, methodological adequacy, interpretability, transparency, applicability, and evidential boundaries.

This review is not intended to validate the empirical results of the initial Instagram study. It evaluates whether the proposed framework is sufficiently clear, defensible, and usable to proceed to manuscript mapping and future empirical validation.

## Review instructions

Please evaluate the framework independently and provide concise justification for each rating. Distinguish between:

- **Essential revision:** required to preserve conceptual or methodological validity;
- **Recommended revision:** materially improves clarity, usability, or reporting;
- **Optional suggestion:** useful but not necessary for the present version.

When possible, identify the relevant document, section, or line. Reviewers should not assume that similarly named metrics are equivalent across platforms.

## Rating scale

- **1 — Inadequate:** major conceptual or methodological revision required
- **2 — Partially adequate:** important revisions required
- **3 — Adequate:** minor revisions or clarifications required
- **4 — Strong:** clear and methodologically defensible
- **N/A — Not assessable:** outside the reviewer’s expertise or insufficient information

## Author response requirements

The CPIF development team should provide a documented response to every essential and recommended revision. Each response should state whether the recommendation was accepted, partially accepted, or not accepted; identify the resulting change and location; and justify any disagreement. Completed reviews and author responses should be preserved with the relevant framework version, subject to reviewer consent and privacy requirements.
