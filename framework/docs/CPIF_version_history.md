# CPIF version history

## Version 1.0.0-draft

- **Date:** 2026-07-13
- **Status:** Draft proposed framework; not externally validated
- **Components created:** conceptual definition; five principles; six-layer specification; interpretation rules; reporting checklist; reproducible scientific figure in SVG, PDF, and PNG; licensing record; structure validator; and validation report.
- **Current limitations:** the framework is based on conceptual synthesis and an initial empirical application; no formal consensus exercise, external methodological assessment, inter-rater evaluation, multicentre study, cross-platform measurement study, or prospective decision-utility evaluation has yet been completed.
- **Next steps:** independent expert review; author confirmation of terminology and licensing; manuscript mapping; preregistered multicentre application; cross-platform assessment; direct measurement of selected effectiveness outcomes; and versioned public release with a persistent identifier.
- **Validation note:** CPIF still requires external validation and multicentre application. The current Instagram study is an empirical demonstration or proof of concept, not definitive validation.
